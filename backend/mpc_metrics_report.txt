F. KNOWN GAPS AFFECTING THE NUMBERS ABOVE
   - Overflow/underflow map (paper eq. 34) is not implemented: 'Overflow px' counts marked pixels
     that leave [0,255]. Recovery is still lossless in the ring Z_2^10, but an 8-bit stego file would wrap.
   - ME/LE are returned as values, not recorded in LSBs (paper eq. 30-33), so EC is not reduced by that overhead.
   - Embedding reveals which pixels sit at the peak bin (r1) to assign bits sequentially; extraction
     reveals r1/r1b per pixel. This is a documented relaxation of the paper's design.
   - n=2 is the validated configuration; n=3 passed a small smoke test; larger n is untested.
